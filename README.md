# Awesome-Semiconductor-Process-Control

## Top Ship Maintenance Management Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Planned Maintenance Systems (PMS), Marine CMMS, Work Orders, Spare Parts, Dry-Dock Planning & Fleet Technical Management*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS/commercial platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Ship Maintenance Management**. These systems help ship owners, managers, and crews plan and execute maintenance, track running hours, manage inventory, generate work orders, and maintain compliance with class and regulatory requirements.



**Examples** include Helm CONNECT, ShipNet, SpecTec AMOS, DANAOS Maintenance, MESPAS, CMMS Marine, NS5 Enterprise, Maximo Marine, Task Assistant Marine, and MariApps (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured marine Planned Maintenance Systems with class-society integration, vessel-specific hierarchies, and maritime regulatory support are predominantly commercial. Open-source options include emerging marine-focused projects (such as SeaVesselManager), general-purpose CMMS platforms that can be adapted, and research/AI prototypes. This section lists every relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Helm CONNECT](https://www.helmoperations.com/)**  

  Modern maritime operations platform with strong planned maintenance, work-order, compliance, and crew modules—popular with harbor, coastal, and growing fleets.



- **[SpecTec AMOS](https://www.spectec.com/)**  

  Long-established maritime maintenance, procurement, and asset-management system widely used for complex planned-maintenance and inventory control.



- **[ShipNet](https://www.shipnet.no/)**  

  Integrated maritime ERP covering technical (PMS), commercial, and financial management for ship owners and managers.



- **[MESPAS](https://www.mespas.com/)**  

  Cloud-oriented marine maintenance and procurement platform focused on fleet technical management and supplier integration.



- **[DANAOS Maintenance / related modules](https://www.danaos.gr/)**  

  Maintenance and technical management capabilities within the broader DANAOS shipping software suite.



- **[ABS Nautical Systems / NS5 Enterprise](https://ww2.eagle.org/)**  

  Class-society-backed fleet management and maintenance solutions used by many owners for technical and compliance workflows.



- **[Maximo Marine (IBM)](https://www.ibm.com/products/maximo)**  

  Industry-specific configuration of IBM Maximo for marine asset and maintenance management.



- **[MariApps, Task Assistant Marine, CMMS Marine](https://www.mariapps.com/)**  

  Specialized marine CMMS and maintenance platforms serving various fleet segments and regional markets.



- **[Other marine PMS & technical management platforms](https://www.helmoperations.com/)**  

  Additional commercial solutions covering dry-dock planning, defect tracking, and fleet-wide maintenance oversight.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[SeaVesselManager](https://seavesselmanager.com/)**  

  Open-source / free Planned Maintenance System designed specifically for the maritime industry, covering fleet overview, time- and running-hour-based maintenance, spare-parts inventory, and related technical management features.



- **[SuperCMMS / Open-Source CMMS](https://github.com/SuperCMMS/Open-Source-CMMS)**  

  Free and open-source Computerized Maintenance Management System backend that organizations can extend for asset, work-order, and preventive-maintenance needs (adaptable to marine use cases).



- **[General open-source CMMS platforms](https://github.com/search?q=CMMS+OR+%22computerized+maintenance%22+open+source)**  

  Community CMMS projects (work orders, preventive maintenance, inventory) that some operators adapt for vessel or shore-workshop maintenance.



- **[Vessel maintenance AI / classification prototypes](https://github.com/search?q=vessel+maintenance+OR+ship+maintenance+AI+OR+maritime+CMMS)**  

  Research and experimental projects applying AI to maintenance records, anomaly alerts, and incident classification for shipping.



- **[Inventory & spare-parts tracking tools](https://github.com/search?q=spare+parts+inventory+OR+maintenance+inventory)**  

  Open-source inventory systems that can support onboard and warehouse spare-parts management.



- **[Running-hours & counter logging helpers](https://github.com/search?q=running+hours+OR+engine+hours+tracker)**  

  Lightweight tools and scripts for capturing equipment running hours that feed maintenance schedules.



- **[Document & certificate management](https://github.com/search?q=certificate+management+OR+compliance+tracking)**

- **[Other maritime technical tools](https://github.com/search?q=maritime+OR+ship+OR+vessel+maintenance+OR+PMS)**

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **ERPNext / Odoo maintenance modules**: General open-source ERP systems with maintenance and asset modules that some smaller operators customize.

- **Checklists & digital forms**: Tools for creating and executing maintenance checklists on tablets or mobiles.

- **Sensor & IoT integration**: Open frameworks (MQTT, Node-RED, etc.) for feeding equipment condition data into maintenance systems.

- **Reporting & dashboards**: Grafana or similar stacks for visualizing maintenance KPIs and overdue work.

- **Class and regulatory reference data**: Public or community-maintained lists that support compliance tracking (used alongside core systems).

- Mobile-first work-order apps built on open-source stacks for crew use.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

For operators seeking an open or low-cost starting point, evaluate **SeaVesselManager** (marine-focused) or a general open-source CMMS that can be configured with vessel hierarchies, running-hour triggers, and spare-parts logistics.  

Full-scale commercial marine PMS platforms (Helm CONNECT, SpecTec AMOS, ShipNet, MESPAS, ABS NS, etc.) provide deep maritime functionality, class integration, procurement workflows, multi-vessel fleet views, and vendor support that most open-source tools do not yet match.  

Many smaller fleets successfully combine open-source or lightweight tools for basic work-order and inventory needs with commercial systems as they grow or face stricter compliance requirements.
